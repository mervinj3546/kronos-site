How it works

Scan, rank, track.

Every setup moves through three stages, and you can always see where each one stands.

  1. 01

    Scan

    Every 15 minutes in market hours, Kronos reads options chains and unusual flow for its ticker universe.

  2. 02

    Rank

    Setups get a direction, a target and a stop from gamma levels and trend structure, then sort by how close and how clean they are.

  3. 03

    Track

    Each pick is saved with its target and stop and scored after the fact, so you can see which setups actually work.

Features

One dashboard. Five ways to read the tape.

GEX Scanner

Gamma levels for every name on your list.

Spot, target, stop, wall and king expiry for each ticker, with the index trend on top so you know if the market agrees.

  • Calls, puts or only setups aligned with trend
  • Same-day touch odds for each target
  • Refreshes on its own during the session

Swing Scanner

From 180 names to the few worth a look.

A funnel shows how many tickers pass each gate: GEX, daily EMA, 30-minute EMA, Keltner position. What is left is ranked.

  • Bullish, bearish and highest-score views
  • Filter summary for every rejected name
  • Market-closed state shows the last scan, never stale data as live

Whale Flow

Big options money, sorted by premium.

Unusual options activity with aggressor detection. Repeat flow groups tickers that keep printing, so one large order doesn't drown out the pattern.

  • Calls, puts, trend-aligned and high-watch filters
  • Volume to open interest on every print
  • Sold-put flow tagged apart from bearish buys

Dot Scanner

Price and volume trend context: which names are being bought or sold with size over 10 and 30 days.

GEX Lookup

Type any ticker for its call wall, put wall, gamma flip and how far price is from each.

Scorecards

Built to be checked.

Every pick is saved with the target and stop it showed. Later, Kronos scores it: did price reach the target, how fast, and before or after the stop. Signals that don't hold up get cut, not hidden.

  • SavedEach pick, as you saw it, with its level and time
  • ScoredTarget first, stop first, or neither by expiry
  • ShownHit rates sit on the page next to the picks

FAQ

Questions

What does Kronos cover?

US stocks and ETFs with listed options. The scanners run on a fixed universe of liquid names; GEX Lookup works on any optionable ticker.

Does Kronos place trades?

No. Kronos is a research dashboard. It doesn't connect to your brokerage account and never sends orders.

How often does the data update?

Scans run every 15 minutes during US market hours. Outside the session, pages show the last completed scan and say so.

Is this investment advice?

No. Kronos shows levels and historical statistics. It does not know your situation, and past hit rates don't promise future results.
